Tonight, we celebrate the spirit, grit, and storytelling soul of the Natural State. From the winding trails of the Ozarks to the neon glow of Little Rock’s streets, Arkansas has long been a quiet muse for filmmakers—local and beyond—who find beauty in its contradictions and power in its people.

This showcase isn’t just about movies. It’s about memory, identity, and the voices that rise from diners, dive bars, cotton fields, and college campuses. Whether you're here for indie gems, student shorts, or cinematic love letters to hometowns, each frame you’ll see tonight carries a piece of Arkansas with it.

So settle in, open your heart, and let these stories take you somewhere familiar—or somewhere entirely new. The reel is rolling, and Arkansas is ready for its close-up.
